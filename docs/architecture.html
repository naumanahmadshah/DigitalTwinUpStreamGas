Gas Upstream Digital Twin - Architecture

Data flows left to right: synthetic OT and IT sources are generated into Unity Catalog, refined through a bronze - silver - gold medallion (serverless Lakeflow / DLT). Genie and an AI/BI dashboard read gold in the lakehouse; the gold marts are also synced into Lakebase (Postgres), which is the single-page application's only database. Every emitter is driven by one seeded field state, so the marts reconcile across production, reliability, emissions and commercial views.

Step 6
Lakebase
The app's only database · scales to zero
Synced tables
every gold mart the app reads, as a Postgres table of the same name (schema digital_twin_app), in one sync pipeline.
Kept fresh
synced right after each medallion load, then every 5 minutes by the “Lakebase sync” job; unchanged marts are skipped.
Write-back ←
the app writes accept / reject / annotate decisions here (audited), each user's preferences (settings) and Genie's cached commercial briefing.
Autoscaling
0.5 to 4 CU; the compute suspends after 5 idle minutes and wakes on the next request.

Step 7
Serving and consumption
How users and agents access it
Databricks Asset Atlas app (from Lakebase)
FastAPI + single-page UI: Command Centre, maps, site views, analytics and reservoir-to-customer impact analysis (with PDF export). Reads and writes Lakebase only; in the background it asks Genie (conversation API) to summarise the top commercial actions.
Genie space (from gold)
“Asset Intelligence Genie”: natural-language analytics over the gold marts only, opened in Databricks One; also writes the app's Commercial & Finance briefing.
AI/BI dashboard (from gold)
published summary of production, reliability and emissions.
AREA-SYSTEM-CLASS-NNNN
One join key ties it all together:
Every tag id (for example CPF002-CMP-AT-1018) decodes to its area, system and equipment class, and resolves through the backbone to its asset, functional location, live value and its ids in every other system (PI historian, EAM, OSDU, P&ID). The app's Asset Explorer demonstrates this resolution live.

Compute

Everything runs on serverless: the generation job, the Lakeflow medallion pipeline, the Lakebase sync pipeline, the SQL warehouse behind Genie and the dashboard, and Lakebase Autoscaling behind the app.

Governance

All lakehouse objects live in one Unity Catalog catalog and schema, and the synced tables in a sibling schema. Tables and columns carry COMMENTs; the app service principal is granted read on the synced marts and read/write on the write-back tables in Lakebase, and may ask the Genie space questions (CAN_RUN, its warehouse and read on gold).

Parameterized

Catalog, schema, warehouse, Lakebase project and the sync schedule are bundle variables, so one asset bundle deploys to any workspace with a single command.

Graceful degradation

Without Genie the header Genie link is simply hidden and the briefing shows a summary worked out from its actions; the app, Genie and the dashboard are independent surfaces over the same gold.
